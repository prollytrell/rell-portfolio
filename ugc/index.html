Good to know

Clear before we create.

Still have a question? Send the brief, or the rough idea, and I’ll tell you what I need.

Does hiring you mean a post on your page?+

No. My core service is content creation: I produce and deliver the files for your brand to publish or run. Creator posting, whitelisting, or exclusivity would only apply if separately agreed in writing.

Which niches do you work in?+

I’m open to most legitimate products and services that fit my voice and values. I’m especially strong with tech, apps, software, and products that benefit from a clear demonstration.

Can the videos be used as ads?+

Yes. Paid and organic usage can both be licensed. Channels, duration, territory, and any exclusivity are confirmed in the project scope before production begins.

What is your turnaround time?+

A standard target is seven business days after the brief and product are in hand. Larger batches, travel, or expedited work are quoted with a specific delivery schedule.
